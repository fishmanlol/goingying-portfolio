This narrow neighborhood park transforms a sloping site into a welcoming place to rest and gather. Striking red shade sails provide shelter and a bold visual identity, while custom cactus-patterned fencing adds character. Along one side, existing elevation changes become playful grassy mounds, inviting children to climb and explore while bringing movement, texture, and unexpected delight to this compact space.
